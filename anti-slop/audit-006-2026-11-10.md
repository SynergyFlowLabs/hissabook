# Anti-slop Audit 006: mobile nav, swipe, and the missing mobile menu (2026-11-10)

Scope: two screenshots sent at 00:18 and 00:21 on the team page and the
overview, reporting three things. Files: `app/globals.css`,
`components/app-sidebar.tsx`, `components/mobile-menu.tsx` (new),
`lib/anti-slop/mobile-overview.test.ts`, `docs/FEATURES.md`.

Rules: same installed set as Audits 001 to 005. Mode: **after**.

## Two of these were regressions I introduced, and I should own that

Both came from the Audit 004 / PR #20 changes, not from older code.

### 1. Bottom nav cut off on /team

Screenshot measurement: a pixel row through both pills gave

| Screen | dark pill from | to |
|---|---|---|
| `/team` | x = **297** | x = **1079** (the last pixel of a 1080px image) |
| overview | x = 158 | x = 917 |

The team pill ran past the right edge and the lime Team item was clipped. The
cause was mine: PR #20 added `overflow-x: auto` to `.mobile-nav` so that five
items plus the bell could scroll on a narrow phone. But the pill was centred
with `left: 50%; transform: translateX(-50%)`. Setting `overflow` on an element
makes it a scroll container, and a scroll container is clipped to its own
padding box around a point (`50%` of the viewport) that sits outside the
element's own box. The result is a pill whose visible slice is offset right of
where it was drawn.

**Fixed** by removing the scroll container entirely and going back to plain
layout: `left: 10px; right: 10px; margin-inline: auto; width: fit-content`. A
fixed element bounded by both insets cannot exceed the viewport whatever its
content does. Six items now fit because the paddings came down (14px to 10px
horizontal, `min-width` 58 to 52, gap 2 to 1), verified by arithmetic in the
audit:

| Viewport | Available | Pill | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 320px | 308 | 244 | fits, 32px margin each side |
| 375px | 363 | 244 | fits, 59px margin |
| 412px | 392 | 339 | fits, 26px margin |

Under 390px the pill tightens to 6px insets, 7px item padding, a 44px floor and
the Menu button drops its label, so a 320px phone keeps six reachable targets
rather than squashing five.

### 2. Quick actions could not be swiped

`touch-action: pan-y` sat on `.quick-actions` since before PR #20, but it only
became a visible bug once the row genuinely overflowed and the owner tried to
reach the hidden items. `pan-y` tells the browser the element only wants
vertical pans, so horizontal gestures were never given to the scroller.

**Fixed**: `touch-action: pan-x pan-y`. The row still scrolls vertically with
the page and now also accepts a horizontal swipe, which is what
`overflow-x: auto` and the snap points were always for.

## 3. Nothing but five destinations existed on mobile

This one was not a regression, it was a gap that had been there all along. The
sidebar is `display: none` below 901px and the bottom pill holds Home,
Activity, Stock, Team and Alerts. So on a phone there was **no route at all** to
`/partners` or `/reports`, no profile view, no sound control and **no way to
sign out**. The sidebar also gets a workspace switcher and an "invite your
accountant" card that had no mobile equivalent.

**Fixed** with `components/mobile-menu.tsx`, opened from a sixth item in the
pill:

- Profile header: avatar initials, name, role and workspace.
- Workspace group: Overview, Transactions, Accounts, Loans & dues.
- Manage group: Inventory, Team & salaries, Partners, Reports.
- Footer: the sound control and Sign out.
- Role gating matches the sidebar exactly (`Team` only for OWNER, ADMIN,
  PAYROLL), and the loans badge carries through.
- Escape closes and returns focus to the opener; focus moves into the sheet on
  open; `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, labelled; scrim click closes; it is
  `display: none` at 901px and up so it can never appear on desktop.
- Anchored above the pill (86px + safe area) and internally scrollable, so a
  short phone cannot trap the sign-out button off screen.
