# Anti-slop Audit 004: mobile Overview screenshot (2026-11-09)

Scope: the owner's Android Chrome screenshot of `/` (1080 x 2280, 412 px CSS
viewport, taken 22:38 local) and the fixes it forced across the Overview screen.
Files touched: `app/dashboard.tsx`, `app/globals.css`, `lib/format.ts`,
`lib/anti-slop/mobile-overview.test.ts`, the five other screens that repeated the
money formatter, and `docs/FEATURES.md`.

Rules: same installed set as Audits 001 to 003. Mode: **after**, driven by pixel
measurements of the attached screenshot rather than by eye.

## How the bug was found (honest method)

No headless browser is available in this sandbox (Chromium cannot link `libnss3`
and Debian mirrors are egress-blocked), so the screenshot itself was measured
with ImageMagick, and the fix was verified by asserting on the compiled CSS in
`.next/static/chunks/*.css` served by a real `next start` on port 3100.

Row scans of the hero panel returned a continuous dark run from **x=42 to
x=1079** (the last pixel of a 1080 px image). The card was not merely wide: it
was laid out to a width wider than the device, and `html { overflow-x: clip }`
silently cut the right edge instead of producing a scrollbar.

Arithmetic confirmed the cause. The six quick-action circles are
`6 x 76 px + 5 x 10 px gaps = 506 px` of min-content inside a horizontal
scroller. Those circles sit in the second column of a `<section className="grid
gap-4 xl:grid-cols-12">`. With no explicit column count on mobile, that grid has
one **`auto`** track, which sizes to the widest item's min-content rather than
to the container. Track = 506 px + 32 px card padding = **538 px**, inside a
412 px viewport. Both cards in the row then drew 538 px wide, which is exactly
what the screenshot shows: the hero chips cut at "SPENT THIS MON…" and the
support note cut mid-word.

## Findings and dispositions

| # | Rule | Pri | Location | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R-02 / R-33 Copy accuracy | HIGH | `app/dashboard.tsx:261` | Heading read "Good morning" in a screenshot whose status bar clock says **22:38** | **Fixed**: `greetingFor()` in `lib/format.ts` bands the hour (05:00 to 11:59 morning, 12:00 to 16:59 afternoon, 17:00 to 04:59 evening) and reads the device clock, which is correct for a client-rendered dashboard. Nine boundary assertions in the new test file. |
| 2 | R-03 Layout at 375 to 412 px | HIGH | `.grid` sections, `app/dashboard.tsx` | Implicit `auto` grid track sized to the 506 px quick-actions row, blowing the overview to 538 px and clipping the hero card | **Fixed**: all three overview sections now declare `grid-cols-1` explicitly (and the six identical sections on `/partners` and `/reports` do too). Belt and braces: `.quick-actions` declares `min-width: 0; max-width: 100%`; `.hero-panel` and `.soft-card` declare `min-width: 0`; `.card-header-row` wraps. |
| 3 | R-03 Hero internals | MEDIUM | `.hero-chips`, `.hero-meta`, `.hero-foot`, `.hero-workspace` | Even inside a correct 412 px card, two chips with `1fr 1fr` and a 14 px padding floor left no room for "SPENT THIS MONTH" | **Fixed**: `minmax(0, 1fr)` tracks, one column under 400 px, `overflow: hidden` on the chip, `.hero-workspace` shrinks with an ellipsis instead of being pushed off screen, and `.hero-foot-note` takes its own full-width line under 560 px. `.hero-amount-row` gained `flex-wrap` so "PKR 519,850" and the reveal button stack rather than shrink. |
| 4 | R-03 Bottom nav occlusion | HIGH | `.content` vs `.mobile-nav` | The "Shared, transparent records" card rendered **under** the pill; its second line was unreadable | **Fixed**: reserve follows the measured pill (7 px + 46 px + 7 px = 60 px, floating 14 px up = 74 px) plus a 20 px gap: `calc(94px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`. The pill also gains `overflow-x: auto` so five items plus the bell can scroll rather than squeeze below their 44 px hit area on a 320 px device. |
| 5 | R-25 Contrast / dead state | HIGH | `.mobile-nav-pill` | The **active** "Home" tab rendered as dark ink on dark green, the only unreadable item in the nav | **Fixed**. This was a genuine specificity bug, not a design choice: `.mobile-nav a > *` (specificity 0,1,1) matched the absolutely positioned pill and overrode `position: absolute` (0,1,0), so the lime pill laid out **inside** the link's grid flow, under the icon. The rule is now `a > *:not(.mobile-nav-pill)`. |
| 6 | R-35 Honesty / status | MEDIUM | Whole product | The hero said `PKR 519,850` and the chips directly under it said `Rs 85,000`: two currencies on one card | **Fixed**: `formatPKR` emits `PKR`, and all 33 remaining `₨` occurrences across 12 files were converted by hand (`app/dashboard.tsx`, reports, transactions, team, inventory, partners, landing page, API error and notification strings, `lib/partners/store.ts`). Masked balances read `PKR ••••••`. The glyph stays only in the explanatory comment in `lib/format.ts`. |
| 7 | R-03 Type floors | MEDIUM | `.hero-chip-sub` 8 px, `.quick-action span` 9 px, `.footer-note` 8.5 px, `.account-kind` 8 px, `.txn-sub` 8.5 px, `.loan-due` 8.5 px, `.owner-note` 8.5 px, `.hero-amount small` 8 px | Captions under a thumb were below a legible floor | **Fixed**: everything the Overview screen renders now sits at 9 px or above, and the new test asserts that no `font-size` in the hero block is under 9 px. |
| 8 | R-24 Affordance | MEDIUM | `.quick-actions` | The row clipped a circle flat at the right edge, which reads as a broken grid, not as a scroller | **Fixed**: a 28 px mask fades the last item, plus scroll snap and `-webkit-overflow-scrolling`. Labels went to 10 px. |
| 9 | Design reference | MEDIUM | `.hero-panel::after` | The decorative ring collided with the translucent circle in the top right of the screenshot | **Not ours.** A 300 px crop at 170% shows a Chrome/Android overlay bubble sitting *above* the page, and the ring is the panel's own decoration at `right: -60px; top: -60px`. Grep across `app/`, `components/` and `lib/` finds no pencil control outside the inventory and team tables. The ring was left as designed; nothing in the diff hides it. |

## What was run, and what was only inspected

- `tsc --noEmit`: clean.
- `npm test`: **75/75**, up from the 63/63 baseline. All 12 new tests are in
  `lib/anti-slop/mobile-overview.test.ts` (greeting bands, money form, grid
  tracks, hero internals, type floor, nav reserve, nav pill specificity,
  scroller affordance).
- `npm run build`: compiled successfully.
- Live `next start` on port 3100 against a fresh SQLite DB, demo login
  (`demo@hisaabook.example`), `/api/dashboard` returned 200 with
  `cashBalance 497250`, `incomeThisMonth 85000`, `expensesThisMonth 50500`.
- Served CSS from that server was grepped for each fix:
  `grid-template-columns: minmax(0,1fr) minmax(0,1fr)` (1),
  `not(.mobile-nav-pill)` (1), `calc(94px` (1), `max-width:400px` (1),
  `max-width:100%` (1). The compiled JS contains `Good evening` and no
  hardcoded `Good morning, `.
- **Not run**: a real 375 px browser pass. The sandbox has no Chromium. The
  layout claims above rest on the screenshot measurements and the source-level
  guards; the live preview on port 3100 is the browser check available to the
  owner.

## Open item for the owner

The `₨` to `PKR` change is user-visible on every screen. It widens some table
columns by roughly one character (for example `PKR 350,000` against
`₨ 350,000`). Worth one glance on `/transactions` and `/reports` at 375 px before
this ships; the tests pin the string form but cannot see a column getting tight.
