Scope: the Partners feature (PR 1 to PR 3), focused on the PR 3 surfaces: the
exit-settlement dialog (`app/partners/partners-client.tsx`), the workspace
movements CSV route, the distribution-history CSV export, the shared `lib/csv.ts`
helper, exited-partner action gating, and the coarse-pointer hit-area fixes.

code/layoutmobile). Mode: **during** (session override). No new visual material
was introduced; everything reuses the approved tokens, `.soft-card`, `.dist-panel`,
`.ghost-pill`, `.text-link`, dialog system and icon set.

Design Read carried over from Audit 001: authenticated bookkeeping app (Next.js),
calm premium fintech language (light mint canvas, deep-ink panels, lime accent),
ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.
