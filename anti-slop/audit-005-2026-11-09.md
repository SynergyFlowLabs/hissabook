# Anti-slop Audit 005: hero card rebuild (2026-11-09)

Scope: the Overview hero card, rebuilt after the owner asked for three removals
and a reference-style card. Files: `app/dashboard.tsx`, `app/globals.css`,
`lib/anti-slop/mobile-overview.test.ts`, `docs/FEATURES.md`.

Rules: same installed set as Audits 001 to 004. Mode: **during**, driven by the
owner's brief and two reference images.

## What the owner asked for

1. Remove "Your books update themselves: every cleared entry, repayment and
   transfer rolls up here automatically."
2. Remove the account avatars in the hero card.
3. Remove "Recorded cleared income" (and, by the same instruction, the matching
   "Recorded cleared expenses" under the spend figure).
4. Make the hero card like the two uploaded reference cards, adapted to this app.

## What the references actually are, and what was taken

Both references are **light** stat cards: a segmented range control, a large
headline number, a small line/area chart, then a short list of labelled figures
with values right-aligned, and one full-width action.

The hero here is deliberately **dark**: it is the balance card, and the dark
panel is the app's existing language, not an accident. Copying the references'
surface would have meant either turning the balance card light (undermining the
visual hierarchy the app already has) or dropping the dark identity the owner
has signed off three times. So the references contributed **structure**, not
palette:

| Reference element | Taken here |
|---|---|
| Segmented 7d / 12d / 30d range control | `.hero-range`, 7d / 30d / 90d, thumb-sized on coarse pointers |
| Large headline number | unchanged: the balance, still the biggest thing in the card |
| Small chart under the number | `.hero-chart`, 92px, area+line, range-driven |
| Short labelled figures with right-aligned values | the two month chips, now label + value only |
| One full-width action | not adopted. The card already sits under a lime "Add transaction" button; a second primary action inside the balance card would compete with it |

## Findings and dispositions

| # | Rule | Pri | Location | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R-24 Dead controls | HIGH | `.hero-range` | A range switch that does not move the chart is a lie | **Wired**: `heroRange` state drives `heroSpark`, seven/30/90-day slices of the same `data.chart` series the bar-chart card uses, so the two controls cannot disagree about what a range means. |
| 2 | R-35 Honesty | HIGH | Hero curve | Daily in/out was flat with four spikes in the demo data and read as broken; cumulative in/out drew two staircases that restated the bar chart below and put two charts on one screen | **Changed to running net movement** after rendering all three candidates against real demo data. Net shows direction, crosses zero legitimately, and does not duplicate the bar chart. The zero line is drawn (`ReferenceLine y=0`) so a negative stretch is visible rather than implied. |
| 3 | R-02 / R-25 Labelling | MEDIUM | `.hero-chart-foot` | A curve with no scale is decoration | **Fixed**: the range dates ("Oct 11 to Nov 9") sit left and the running total ("Net +PKR 80,000", lime when positive, terra when negative) sits right. The tooltip states the net at any hovered day, and honours the existing hide-balance toggle (`PKR ••••••`). |
| 4 | R-03 Layout at 375px | HIGH | `.hero-chart`, `.hero-range` | New elements are the usual source of a fresh overflow | **Guarded**: `.hero-chart` declares `min-width: 0` and a 92px height so it can never eat the card; range buttons are 30px on fine pointers and 40px on coarse; the foot wraps. The existing grid guards from Audit 004 still hold and the suite covers the new height. |

## Deliberately kept

- The workspace chip ("Safa Studio Demo") and the "Across 2 accounts ·
  Creative agency · Cleared records only" line. The owner did not ask for these,
  and they answer "whose money is this" and "what is counted", which the audit
  for Audit 004 established are load-bearing.
- The decorative ring behind the top-right corner (the pencil bubble in the
  owner's screenshots is a Chrome overlay, established in Audit 004).

## What was run, and what was only inspected

- `tsc --noEmit`: clean.
- `npm test`: **76/76** (63 baseline, 12 from Audit 004, 1 added here for the
  hero curve).
- `npm run build`: compiled successfully; the new CSS is present in the served
  chunk (`hero-range button.active`, `.hero-chart{...height:92px}`, the coarse
  pointer override) and `hero-spark-fill` is in the client bundle.
- Fresh `next start` on 3100 with demo login; `/api/dashboard` returns 90 chart
  points and cumulative demo figures; net per range computed from that payload:
  7d +34,000, 30d +20,000, 90d +80,000.
- **Rendered the curve offline**: no browser and no SVG delegate exist here, so
  the three candidate geometries were rasterised from the real API payload with
  a small line rasteriser and compared. That comparison is what ruled out daily
  in/out and cumulative in/out.
- **Not run**: a real browser pass. The card has not been seen rendered at
  375px. That remains the owner's check.

## Note for the owner

The demo workspace books about four transaction days in 90, so the hero curve is
visibly step-like in the public demo. That is the data, not the chart: a real
workspace with steady entries draws a smooth line. If the demo matters as a
showcase, seeding a few more spread-out entries would make it look alive.
